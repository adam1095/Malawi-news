Pofika usiku wathawu, patha masiku atatu Magetsi asakuyaka pachipatala cha Boma cha Mangochi. Anthu ena omwe abale awo agonekedwa pa chipatalachi ati mayunitsi amagetsiwa anatha lachiwiri masana ndipo pofika dzulo madzulo anali asanayakebe..

Ogwira ntchito ena pachipatalachi ati vuto la magetsili lasokoneza ntchito yawo yothandiza anthu odwala omwe amafuna kupatsidwa mpweya owathandizira kupuma. "Usiku wathawu (Lachinayi) ana atatu amwalira chifukwa sitinakwanitse kuwaika pa makina owathandiza kupuma kamba ka vuto la Magetsi. Pamasiku atatuwa tataya miyoyo ya anthu angapo kamba ka vutoli," atero ogwira ntchito ena omwe tinawapeza pachipatalachi..

Koma nkulu wa ntchito za umoyo m'boma la Mangochi Henry Chibowa wati magetsiwa anatha chifukwa pa pachipatalachi panaikidwa makina okonzera mpweya opumila(Oxygen)omwe ndi ofuna mphamvu zambiri zaagetsi.
